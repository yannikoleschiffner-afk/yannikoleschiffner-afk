## Yannik Schiffner

I'm doing an MSc in Finance at NUS in Singapore, after a business degree at LMU Munich. Below are the projects I've built so far.

**[Retail order execution quality at U.S. wholesalers](https://github.com/yannikoleschiffner-afk/us-equity-execution-quality)**
My bachelor thesis (grade 1.0). The usual assumption is that Citadel and Virtu give retail orders the best prices because they're the biggest. I went through two years of SEC Rule 605 reports, and a smaller firm, G1X Susquehanna, did better almost every time.

**[Greenwashing, ESG ratings and stock returns](https://github.com/yannikoleschiffner-afk/greenwashing-esg-stock-returns)**
A group project at NUS. My part was the code that reads about 7,500 S&P 500 annual reports with FinBERT and scores how green each company sounds. What surprised us was the ESG rating. It follows how much a firm talks about the environment more closely than what its emissions actually do.

**[Insider trading detection on prediction markets](https://github.com/yannikoleschiffner-afk/prediction-market-insider-detection)**
A bot that watches large trades on Polymarket and flags wallets that look like they know something, usually a brand-new account putting a lot of money on one market. It can follow those trades and handle the exits itself. I'd still call it a research project.

**[AI agent for a Kaggle market simulation](https://github.com/yannikoleschiffner-afk/kaggle-market-simulation-agent)**
My entry for Kaggriculture, a two-player farming game with a market that reacts to what both players sell. It finished in the top 100 of more than 10,000 entrants. Most of the hours went into replaying games I lost and working out why.

I mostly work in Python (pandas, statsmodels, Hugging Face), plus R and Excel/VBA.
